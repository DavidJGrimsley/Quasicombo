# Quasicombo blockout playtest

Open `/Game/Maps/Lvl_Main` in Unreal 5.8.3 and play as one local player. The three sections expose A (top), B (middle), and C (bottom) choices. Repeating a lane is valid, including A1 → A2. Crossing a leg's entry commits the choice; one `RearGateSection1/2/3` mesh closes behind all three lanes after the player clears it, the unchosen forward exits stay sealed, and the chosen forward exit opens when every assigned enemy dies or falls away. The final leg transfers the player to the shared mannequin boss arena.

Place `/Game/Quasicombo/Enemies/BP_QC_Enemy` manually and set its **Species** and **Tier** in **Quasicombo → Variant**. Wood monsters take 3/4/5 accepted strikes and deal 1.0 / 2.0 / 3.0 damage at light/medium/heavy; stone golems take 4/5/6 strikes and deal 1.5 / 3.0 / 4.5 damage. Both families scale to 1.1/1.2375/1.375 after the 10% size increase. Their overhead health bars show remaining strike health; detection reaches 800 cm, melee reach is 150 cm, cooldown is 1.0 s, and strike montages play at 1.2× speed. Add each placed actor to its lane entry's **Quasicombo → Encounter → Enemies** array. An empty array opens that lane's exit as soon as it is selected. The lizard boss deals 2.0 damage per attack and 4.0 damage for its tail sweep; it still takes six strikes. Strike six begins a three-prompt finisher. Each prompt now has four real-time seconds for testing. The HUD reads the current input mapping for Attack, Dash, and Jump, and draws Xbox-style A/B/X/Y or shoulder/trigger icons for mapped gamepad buttons. The side-scroller defaults are X for Attack, RB for Dash, and A for Jump. The icons are drawn in UMG/Slate; no button-art download is required. Pit death, combat death, or a failed prompt reloads the entire map; a successful finisher wins. The Quantum API plugin is enabled and accepts a development key through **Project Settings → Quantum API**. The checked-in setting has an empty key. If a request fails, the matching validated route fixture supplies the initial state; evolution failure keeps the last valid complex state.

The side-scroller player starts with 10 HP. The overhead bar is 30% larger; the numeric HP readout has been removed from the HUD. After taking damage, health stays unchanged for three seconds, then recovers at 1.0 HP per second (10% of maximum), stopping at full health. Another hit restarts the delay. In PIE, check the overhead bar's visible size and recovery.

The HUD shows the unlabeled elapsed-time counter and Best Combo at top left. Keep the separate `UBP_GameHUD` combo count and its countdown. The A/B/C drawing stays at the right with Vacuum/Tau, Routes, and Braid beneath it; probabilities show `--` before a quantum state exists. After a successful finisher, the top-centered score contains time, combo, net braid, and sampled quantum points. The three victory options open technical info and a scrolling credits roll, leave Replay with Cannon Blaster as a visible no-op, and Quit. Test mouse, keyboard, and gamepad navigation at both 1920x1080 and a narrow window. Edit `/Game/UI/DA_QuasicomboCredits` in the Unreal Content Browser when credits change; `Project/credits.md` supplied the initial three entries.

After changing C++, close this project in Unreal Editor and build `Braided_Quanta2026Editor Win64 Development`. Visual Studio can remain open with debugging stopped. Keep the other project's Unreal sessions undisturbed. The reusable editor scripts are `SetupQuasicomboEnemy.py`, `CreateQuasicomboAssets.py` (boss only), `WireQuasicomboLevel.py`, `CompileQuasicomboBlueprints.py`, and `VerifyQuasicomboLevel.py`. `WireQuasicomboLevel.py` updates route actors by their `QC_` labels and saves the level; it never creates enemies or changes entry enemy arrays.

The automated `Quasicombo` suite covers all 27 route histories, gate/encounter/pit/arena behavior, accepted strikes, player health increase and regeneration, a real enemy approach and attack, boss strike-six victory, QTE timing/interruption, and quantum fixtures/callback guards.
